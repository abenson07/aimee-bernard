Care

Made to be kept. Kept how.

I.

Rinse the leash

Salt eats urethane slowly. A rinse in the car park tap and a loose coil in the boot, never a knot, and it lasts as long as the board.

II.

Keep the fins in the sock

The tint is in the resin, not on it, but the edge is hand-foiled and the edge is the fin. Off the board and into the sock between sessions.

III.

Wax by water

Cold at home, cool by late summer, the warm blocks for trips. The wrong block in the wrong water is the only time wax fails.

IV.

Send it back

If a fin breaks in normal use we replace it and pay the post. If it hit a rock, we repair it at cost and tell you what it hit.
