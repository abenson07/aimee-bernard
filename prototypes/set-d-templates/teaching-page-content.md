## 4. CU Graduate School

### MIMS7530. Introduction to Immunology — Course Director. 2021-current
Introductory course providing foundation in immunology for students with general biology and cell biology backgrounds. Topics include:
- Cells, Organs, and Microenvironments of the Immune System
- Innate Immunity, Inflammation, and the Complement System
- Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) and Antigen Processing & Presentation
- T cell Development, Activation, and Effector Functions
- Antibody Structure & Function
- B cell Development, Activation and Differentiation
- Antigen Receptor Signaling (BCR and TCR)
- Type I-IV Hypersensitivities
- Immune Response to Cancer & Immunotherapy
- Autoimmunity & Tolerance
- Vaccines and the Immune Response

### MIMS6062. Introduction to Science Communication — Course Director. 2026-current
Designed to introduce skills for conveying complex scientific concepts to diverse audiences including public, policymakers, and scientists from different fields. Through lectures, in-class activities and practical assignments, students learn:
- Key principles of clear and accurate scientific communication
- Ethics of public science discourse
- Strategies for engaging written, media and digital platforms
- Adapting messages for different target audiences
- Crafting compelling narratives
- Developing visual aids

## 6. CU Denver University Honors & Leadership Program
**Course Director. 2015-2021**

### UNHL4820. Scientific Thinking
Intensive analysis of primary and secondary literature across sciences designed to develop critical thinking, content integration, and analysis skills. Through reading, group work, oral and written presentations, and class discussion, students:
- Expand understanding and appreciation of the scientific method
- Develop ability to critically analyze and evaluate experimental design in scientific and social contexts
